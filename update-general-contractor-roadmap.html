The General Contractor Roadmap: From Laborer to Licensed GC

The comprehensive milestone roadmap our senior fellows follow to progress from on-site construction workers to seven-figure licensed general contractors.

Justin Ortiz
Founder & Master Contractor, Operation GO

How does a high school apprentice grow into a licensed General Contractor managing seven-figure municipal projects? It starts with mastering sub-trade coordination, permit filing, OSHA compliance, and construction escrow management.

The Five Milestones of General Contracting Mastery

Transitioning from a tradesperson who works with their hands to an executive who manages multi-million dollar job sites requires a clear, deliberate path:

  1. Field Credentialing (Years 1–2): OSHA-30, CPR/First Aid, and multi-trade journeyman experience in framing, electrical, and plumbing rough-ins.
  2. Estimating & Takeoffs (Years 2–3): Mastering PlanSwift, Procore, and Bluebeam Revu to calculate exact material quantities, labor hours, and sub-bids.
  3. Permit & Code Filing (Year 3): Working directly with local municipal building departments to secure zoning variances and building permits.
  4. Bonding & Insurance (Year 4): Establishing commercial liability coverage, builder’s risk policies, and bonding capacity for public works.
  5. Project Management & Escrow (Year 5+): Directing simultaneous multi-trade job sites, managing draw schedules, and delivering projects on time and on budget.
