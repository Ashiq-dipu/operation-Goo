OPERATION GO DO GOOD ROCHESTER NY

Helping People In Need Around The World

Founded in Rochester, NY by RCSD graduate and master contractor Justin Ortiz, Operation GO™ is a community-driven skilled trades and creative workforce foundry equipping urban youth ages 12–18 with OSHA credentials, paid apprentice stipends, and debt-free generational careers.

Tuition-Free Vocational Education

100% free OSHA-10 certified instruction across commercial carpentry, bespoke footwear, cinema production, and structural trades.

Living Wages & Union Pipelines

Direct placement into union apprenticeships (Carpenters Local 276, electrical, framing) paying $25–$45/hr starting wages with zero loan debt.

Program to Empower Others

Comprehensive vocational mastery equipping youth for life-long career independence
