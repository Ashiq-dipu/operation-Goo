Construction & Commercial Carpentry

Where carpentry becomes a living-wage career. Students master wood framing, power tools, safety protocols, and structural craftsmanship under licensed master contractors.

✓
Curriculum: 2x4 and 2x6 timber framing, roof trusses, miter and circular saws, levels, laser measuring, and blueprint geometry.
✓
Facility Scope: Delivered at our main Rochester vocational workshop, community park build sites, and secure juvenile justice facility partnerships.
✓
Union Placement: Direct pipeline into Carpenters Local 276 paying $25–$45/hr starting scale with full pension and medical benefits.

Summer Youth Employment Program (SYEP)

A 6–8 week paid summer intensive where youth build real community architecture and earn hourly wages, culminating in an acclaimed public showcase covered by WHAM13 and WXXI.

✓
Curriculum: 6–8 week intensive field timber framing, community gazebo & pavilion builds, power tool safety, and public architectural engineering.
✓
Facility Scope: Delivered across Rochester municipal parks, urban garden sites, and public neighborhood pavilions.
✓
Union Placement: Direct pipeline into pre-apprenticeship union training and private commercial framing contractors.

Apparel & Sneaker Reconstruction Lab

Students learn industrial footwear deconstruction, pattern drafting, leatherworking, heavy sewing machine operation, and independent streetwear brand creation.

✓
Curriculum: Complete footwear teardown, lasting boards, outsole unbonding, skiving, leatherwork, and specialized contact adhesive chemistry.
✓
Facility Scope: Hands-on industrial design studio equipped with heavy walking-foot sewing machines, heat presses, and professional airbrush systems.
✓
Career Placement: Direct pipeline into industrial prototyping, retail footwear pop-ups, and bespoke brand incubators.

4K Cinema Video Production & Media

Equipping youth with commercial cinema cameras, 3-point studio lighting, boom audio capture, and Hollywood-grade DaVinci Resolve editing and color grading.

✓
Curriculum: Camera sensor operation, optics, 3-point studio lighting, Foley boom audio, DaVinci Resolve color grading, and video editing.
✓
Facility Scope: Delivered in our Rochester production soundstage, audio recording booth, and live community field documentary shoots.
✓
Industry Placement: Advanced students shoot paid commercial video reels for local businesses and build verified portfolios for creative agencies.

Real Estate & Financial Literacy

Demystifying property acquisition, credit building, mortgage math, and structural property underwriting so Rochester youth can build long-term generational wealth.

✓
Curriculum: Banking mechanics, compound interest math, credit defense, opening custodial accounts, and avoiding predatory loans.
✓
Facility Scope: Hands-on site walkthroughs assessing structural rehab scopes, electrical safety, plumbing, and estimating renovation budgets.
✓
Generational Placement: Direct pathways to purchase, restore, and retain equity in Rochester multi-family homes through municipal grant programs.

Biz Brain Youth Entrepreneurship & Pitch Lab

Teens draft executable business plans, calculate unit margins, master customer discovery, and compete in live pitch events for micro-grant seed capital.

✓
Curriculum: Unit cost economics, pricing strategy, break-even calculations, pitch deck design, and margin protection for student ventures.
✓
Facility Scope: Weekly incubator lab with authentic Rochester angel investors, banking executives, and civic founders.
✓
Commercial Placement: Practical business formation filing for DBAs, EINs, commercial banking accounts, and local contractor licensing.

Reprogram Mindset, Public Speaking & Civics

Overcoming trauma barriers, instilling personal discipline, mastering executive communication, and speaking powerfully in municipal boardrooms.

✓
Curriculum: Conquering stage fright, mastering vocal modulation, emotional regulation, and crafting compelling keynote presentations.
✓
Facility Scope: Small-group mentor circles delivering personal accountability, active listening, and executive workplace etiquette.
✓
Civic Placement: Direct interface with Rochester City Council, Monroe County committees, and community leadership boards.

Academic Support & Trades Credit Recovery

Partnered with Rochester City School District to provide contextualized trades math, Regents exam coaching, and academic recovery to guarantee graduation.

✓
Curriculum: Pythagorean theorem, layout angles, board-footage formulas, and Regents exam coaching learned through practical carpentry.
✓
Facility Scope: Certified educators provide 1-on-1 tutoring at our Rochester learning lab integrated with vocational shop classes.
✓
Diploma Placement: Earn credited elective and vocational units recognized toward high school diplomas and guaranteed graduation.
