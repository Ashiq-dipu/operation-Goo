WE THINK OUTSIDE OF THE BOX WHEN IT COMES TO EDUCATION, AND WE BELIEVE THAT OUR PROGRAMS ARE THE FUTURE OF EDUCATION.

Rochester’s youth don’t need more promises—they need real trades that build generational futures. Operation GO bridges classroom education with hands-on timber framing, custom sneaker and apparel labs, 4K cinema studios, and union career placements with zero student debt.
