How We Take You from Zero Experience to Living-Wage Mastery.

A proven, structured curriculum engineered to ensure financial independence without carrying a single dollar of student debt.

01 • DAY 1 TO 30

Discovery & Pro Toolset Induction

Complete your aptitude assessment, select your vocational guild, and receive your brand-new Milwaukee pro toolset, personal safety PPE, and cohort uniform at zero cost.

✓ Zero Out-of-Pocket Cost • Complete Tool Kit
02 • MONTHS 2 TO 6

Hands-On Guild Apprenticeship

Clock active build hours on residential jobs, obtain OSHA-10 safety certifications, design custom sneakers or media campaigns, and earn $16/hr during paid build shifts.

✓ Earn While You Learn • Real Client Projects
03 • GRADUATION & BEYOND

Direct Union Signing & Career Launch

Direct transition into Local 276 Carpenters, electrical unions, or enterprise incubation. Start earning $28–$45/hr with full health, pension, and lifetime brotherhood.

✓ Guaranteed Signing Pathways • $28–$45/hr
01 / 06

MENTOR THE NEXT GENERATION OF BUILDERS

Master contractors & artisans: earn $45–$65/hr training small cohorts in Rochester.
